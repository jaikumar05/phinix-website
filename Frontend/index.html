Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Phinix Modular Furniture

Why choose modular kitchens?

Modular kitchens are budget-friendly, long-lasting, easy-to-use kitchens that can be customised to your preference. They are easy to install and can be dismantled and moved to new homes easily.

How Do I Choose A Good Modular Kitchen Design in Jaipur?

Understand your kitchen layout, list your requirements and pick a modular kitchen design depending on your budget and requirements. You can also get in touch with an expert interior designer from Phinix, who can recommend and craft the perfect modular kitchen for your home.

What are The Benefits of a Modular Kitchen?

Modular kitchens are customisable, easy to maintain, come with a variety of options for all types of budgets and are easy to install.

What type of Material and finish is best for a Modular Kitchen?

For budget-friendly kitchens, try MDF or plywood with acrylic finishes. Go for HDMR materials and glass finishes for higher durability and aesthetic purposes.

What Points to Consider During Modular Kitchen Installation?

Installing a modular kitchen is a hassle-free experience. The units are factory-made and pre-assembled, they just have to be installed in your space. Keep your kitchen bare and clean for easy installation. Additionally, have the modular kitchen units installed before working on the plumbing and electrical aspects of your kitchen.

Can modular kitchens be customized?

Yes, they can be fully customized as per the size, shape, color preferences, and appliance requirements.

How long does it take to install a modular kitchen?

Installation usually takes 30–45 days depending on the size and complexity.

What materials are best for wardrobes, vanity units, and doors?

Wardrobes, vanity units, and doors are usually made from high-quality materials like HDF or HDH MR with laminate finish, solid wood or WPC acrylic. For wardrobes, laminated plywood or HDF is preferred due to strength and easy maintenance. Vanity units often use waterproof materials like MDF or marine plywood to withstand moisture. For doors, solid wood and flush doors are popular choices as they balance durability with modern aesthetics.

Can wardrobes, vanity units, and doors be customized to match home interiors?

Yes, they can be fully customized to match your home interiors. From size, color, and finish to handles, hinges, and storage layout, every detail can be designed to suit your personal style and functional needs. Modern modular options also allow seamless integration with bedrooms, bathrooms, and living spaces while maintaining a stylish and cohesive look.

How to care for my modular kitchen?

Wipe cabinets and countertops daily with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals and excess water, especially on wooden modules. Keep chimney filters clean for better ventilation. Gently use drawers and shutters to prevent wear. Check hinges, channels, and gas lines regularly to ensure smooth, safe, and long-lasting performance. Clean them every 15–30 days for best performance.
