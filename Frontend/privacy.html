Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Phinix Modular Furniture (“Phinix”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be involved when you visit this website and how it is used.

Information you provide

You may choose to contact us by phone or email. If you do, we receive the contact details and message you provide so we can respond to your enquiry. The consultation and brochure forms on this website currently provide an on-page confirmation and do not send or save the entered details.

Information collected by the website

Our website is primarily informational. The web hosting provider may process standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and request time, to deliver and protect the site. We do not use this website to make automated decisions about visitors.

Cookies and third-party services

This site does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools. Some page assets, such as fonts or images, may be loaded from third-party services. Those providers may receive technical information from your browser when the assets load; their own privacy policies apply.

How we use information

Information you share directly with us is used to respond to your request, discuss furniture or design services, and provide customer support. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related business records. We use reasonable safeguards, though no website or electronic transfer can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide personal information through this site and can contact us to ask about information you have shared directly with us, or request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our site or practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with its updated date.
