Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Phinix Modular Furniture (“Phinix”, “we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be involved when you visit our website and how it is used, stored, and protected.

Information you provide

You may choose to contact us by phone, email, or through the consultation and brochure forms on this website. If you do, we receive the contact details and message you provide so we can respond to your enquiry. The forms on this website currently provide an on-page confirmation and do not permanently store or transmit the entered details to a third-party database.

Information collected automatically

Our website is primarily informational. Our web hosting provider may process standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and request time, to deliver and protect the site. We do not use this website to make automated decisions about visitors, and we do not track individuals across third-party websites.

Cookies and third-party services

This site does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools. Some page assets, such as fonts or images, may be loaded from third-party services (for example, Google Fonts or image hosts). Those providers may receive technical information from your browser when the assets load; their own privacy policies apply.

How we use information

Information you share directly with us is used only to respond to your request, discuss furniture or interior design services, provide customer support, and send you information you have asked for. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to any third party.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related business records. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, though no website or electronic transfer can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask that your details be removed from our records by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to genuine requests within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
